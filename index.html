BlackButWhyte

Natural history wall art. One specimen, drawn large, named in Latin underneath.

Prints in the tradition of the old natural history plates: a single subject, alone on the page, with clean space on all four sides so it sits beside whatever is already on your wall rather than arguing with it.

Printed in Florida on 189 gsm museum-grade archival paper with pigment inks. Two sizes, 8×10 and 11×14, both standard frame sizes. Free shipping within the United States.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026. Operated by Poised for BlackButWhyte.

Who this covers

This policy covers the BlackButWhyte website and the private Pinterest integration operated by Poised. That integration is a single-operator tool. It has no other users and offers no service to the public.

What the Pinterest integration does

It connects to one Pinterest account: the one belonging to the shop owner. It creates Pins and boards on that account for products published in our own Etsy shop, and it reads back analytics for those Pins so we can see which of our own performed.

Data we access and store

  • Pinterest access tokens, stored encrypted by Make.com, the automation platform the integration runs on.
  • Identifiers and analytics for our own Pins, stored in a private Airtable base that only the owner can open.
  • Product data from our own Etsy shop — titles, descriptions, images and listing links.

Data belonging to other people

The integration does not request, access, store or process data belonging to any Pinterest user other than its owner. It reads no other Pinner's Pins or boards, and it has no mechanism to do so.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party. Nothing retrieved from Pinterest is displayed publicly or used anywhere outside the owner's own private workflow. We do not serve advertising and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking on this page.

Buyers

Purchases are handled entirely by Etsy under Etsy's own privacy policy, and fulfilment is handled by Printify under theirs. We receive only the order information Etsy provides in order to fulfil an order, and we do not collect payment details at any point.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only while the integration is in use, and is deleted when the integration is disconnected or on request. To request deletion of any data we hold, email poisedautomation@gmail.com and we will action it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
